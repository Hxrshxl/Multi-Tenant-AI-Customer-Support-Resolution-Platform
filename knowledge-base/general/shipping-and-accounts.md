# Shipping and account basics

## Where we ship

We ship to the UK, Ireland and the EU. Orders to Ireland and the EU may attract
import duties, which are the recipient's responsibility.

## Adding a user to your account

Account owners can invite users under **Account > Team**. Invited users receive
an email valid for 7 days. An expired invitation can simply be re-sent.

## Support hours

Support is staffed 09:00 to 17:30 UK time, Monday to Friday, excluding public
holidays. Messages outside those hours are answered the next working day.
