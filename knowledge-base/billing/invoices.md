# Invoices and VAT

## Finding an invoice

Every invoice is available under **Account > Billing > Invoices**. Invoices are
generated on the first working day after a billing period closes.

## Requesting a VAT invoice

If your account has a VAT number on file, invoices include it automatically. If
the number was added after an invoice was issued, contact support with the
invoice number and a corrected copy will be reissued within one working day.

## Why a price changed

Subscription prices change for three reasons: a plan change you made, a seat
count change, or an annual price review. Price reviews are announced at least
30 days before they take effect.
