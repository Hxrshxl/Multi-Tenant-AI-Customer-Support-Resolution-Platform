# Duplicate charges

## What causes them

A duplicate charge is almost always a failed payment that was retried. The
first attempt appears as a pending authorisation and clears within 5 working
days without ever being captured.

## How to confirm

Compare the two amounts. A genuine duplicate has identical amounts and
timestamps within a few minutes of each other. Different amounts usually mean a
plan change was prorated.

## Resolution

Confirmed duplicates are refunded in full to the original payment method.
Refunds take 5 to 10 working days to appear, depending on the issuing bank.


## Partial refunds

Partial refunds are issued when only part of an order is returned. The refunded
amount excludes original shipping unless the whole order is returned.
